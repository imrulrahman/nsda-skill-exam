Account & Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete your NSDA Skill Exam account and the personal information associated with it. Send a request using the button below, then follow the verification steps.

Email an account deletion request

Please note: Sending an email starts a request; it does not automatically delete your account or data. Deletion occurs only after we review and verify the request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email mahmud.bd7@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request – NSDA Skill Exam.
  2. In the message, include the email address used for your app account and, if available, your name or learner profile identifier. Do not include your password.
  3. Reply to any follow-up questions so we can confirm that you own or are authorized to manage the account.
  4. We will review the request and let you know if we need more information. Once verified, we will process the deletion and confirm when it is complete.

Verification and timing

For your protection, we may verify your identity by replying to the account email address or asking for limited information already associated with your account. We will not ask you to send your password.

We aim to complete verified requests within 7–14 business days. This is an estimate, not a guarantee; requests that need additional verification or involve service-provider processing may take longer. We will contact you if there is a delay.

What deletion covers

After a request is verified and processed, we will delete or de-identify the account and associated personal data under our control, including learning progress and related training records where applicable. Data held by service providers on our behalf will also be addressed as applicable.

Some records may remain for a limited period when needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect the security of the App, or prevent fraud and abuse. Any retained information will be restricted to that purpose and deleted when no longer needed.

Need help?

Questions about an account deletion request? Contact us at mahmud.bd7@gmail.com.
