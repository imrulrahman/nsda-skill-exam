Effective date: November 6, 2026 · Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NSDA Skill Exam Preparation (“the App”, “we”, “our” or “us”) handles information when you use the App. The App is designed to help learners prepare for skills assessment and examination-related topics.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the services enabled in the App, the following information may be processed:

  • Account information: information used to create or sign in to an account, such as your email address and authentication identifiers, if account features are enabled.
  • Learning and progress information: practice activity, answers, scores, bookmarks and learning progress, where these features are available and synchronized.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, app version, language, diagnostic information and technical identifiers needed for app operation, security and troubleshooting.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers and ad interaction data may be processed by Google AdMob to display and measure advertisements.

We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information unless a specific App feature clearly requires it. Please do not enter sensitive information into free-text fields.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide app features, authenticate users, save learning progress, maintain and improve the App, diagnose errors, protect the service against misuse, and display or measure advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase – may be used for authentication, cloud data storage and app analytics. Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google AdMob – may be used to serve advertisements and measure ad performance. How Google uses information from apps.
  • Google Play services – may provide app distribution, security and related platform services. Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above to provide their services, or disclosed where required by law, to protect users and the App, or with your consent.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can stop using the App at any time. If the App provides account features and you want your account or associated personal data deleted, contact us using the developer contact email shown on the App’s Google Play listing. We may need to verify your request. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security or legitimate operational purposes.

You can also manage or reset your device’s advertising preferences through your Android or Google settings. You may limit or personalize ads where those controls are available.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the App after a policy update means the updated policy applies to your subsequent use.

9. Contact Us

For questions, privacy requests or concerns about this policy, contact the developer through the support email address provided on the NSDA Skill Exam Preparation Google Play Store listing.

This policy should be reviewed against the exact data collected and SDKs enabled in the published app and the Google Play Console Data safety form.
